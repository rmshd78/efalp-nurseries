Our nurseries

Two sites in Fujairah, each set up for the plants it grows. Visits can be arranged for customers who want to select their trees in person.

Al Raheeb Nursery

Al Raheeb, Fujairah, UAE

75species
0named varieties

Our main production site. Climate-controlled greenhouses hold the tropical and specialty collections, while shade houses and a net house raise fruit trees, palms, flowering trees and native species.

Growing units

  • Greenhouse 1Cocoa, coffee, mango varieties and Brazilian cherry
  • Greenhouse 2Mangoes, passion fruit, olives, cocoa, coconut, black pepper and navel orange
  • Greenhouse 3Cocoa
  • Shade house 1Sidr, mixed fruit trees, grapes, citrus, neem, bodhi tree and chinaberry
  • Shade houses 2 to 4New high structures for flowering trees, exotic palms, small trees, shrubs and groundcovers
  • Net houseGhaf, sidr, date palms, Mexican fan palms, bamboo, aloe vera and young flowering trees

    Dhadna Nursery

    Dhadna, Fujairah, UAE

    45species
    0named varieties

    Dhadna focuses on grafted fruit trees, especially mangoes from India, Pakistan, Egypt and the Gulf, and on propagation, raising new native and shade trees from seed and cuttings.

    Growing units

    • Shade houseGrafted mangoes, pomegranates, citrus, grapes, olives, sidr, avocado and desert plants
    • Greenhouse 1Mango rootstocks and young grafts, pomegranates and navel oranges
    • Greenhouse 2Propagation from seed: neem, ghaf, golden shower, royal poinciana, yellow flame tree, acacia, argan and more
    • Cuttings unitPomegranate, olive, neem, jasmine, pink trumpet tree and ficus raised from cuttings
