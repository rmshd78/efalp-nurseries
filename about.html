Company profile

Efalp Nurseries is the nursery division of Emirates Farm for Livestock & Agriculture Production L.L.C. (EFALP), growing trees and plants for the UAE at Al Raheeb and Dhadna in Fujairah.

Who we are

We propagate, graft and grow plants for orchards, farms, landscape projects, public spaces and private estates. Our range covers 94 species, from more than forty mango varieties and regional pomegranates to the UAE's own ghaf and sidr, ornamental flowering trees, palms and greenhouse-grown cocoa and coffee.

Both nurseries sit in the emirate of Fujairah on the UAE's east coast. Al Raheeb is the larger site with greenhouses, shade houses and a net house; Dhadna specialises in grafted fruit trees and in raising new trees from seed and cuttings.

Plants are grown under the supervision of our agricultural engineers, with each batch recorded by source and planting date.

Our parent company

Emirates Farm (EFALP) was founded in Abu Dhabi in 2009 as a small, home-grown agricultural project. A dedicated research and development team helped turn desert land into productive farms, introducing net-house cultivation to improve yields while reducing water use.

Today EFALP is one of Abu Dhabi's leading producers of fruit, vegetables, mushrooms, flowers and cheese. Its Manchego-style and spicy Toma cheeses won Platinum at the Dubai International Taste Awards 2024.

What guides us

Sustainability

We favour native and water-wise species and grow under net and shade to save water, in line with the UAE's agricultural goals.

Quality

Plants are propagated from selected stock, grafted on proven rootstocks and grown on until they are ready for the field.

Transparency

We record the source and planting date of every batch and share the origin of the plants you buy.

Where our stock comes from

We source seed, budwood and young plants from trusted growers in the UAE and abroad, then grow them on in Fujairah until they are established. Each plant in the catalogue lists the countries its stock came from.
