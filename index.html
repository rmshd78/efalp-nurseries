A nursery backed by Emirates Farm

Emirates Farm for Livestock & Agriculture Production (EFALP) began in Abu Dhabi in 2009 as a home-grown agricultural venture. It pioneered net-house farming to grow more with less water in desert conditions, and today supplies fresh produce, mushrooms, flowers and award-winning cheeses across the UAE.

Efalp Nurseries brings that experience to horticulture. Our teams in Fujairah propagate, graft and grow trees and plants that are proven in local heat and soils, for farms, landscape projects, municipalities and private estates.

How we grow

Practices that give every plant a strong start once it is planted out.

Grafted on proven rootstocks

Mangoes are grafted onto hardy Qirat and Sukkari rootstocks, Thai sidr onto wild sidr and citrus onto vigorous narenj, for stronger roots in local soils.

Protected cultivation

Greenhouses, shade houses and net houses let us raise tropical crops such as cocoa and coffee and harden young trees off before they leave the nursery.

Native species from seed

Ghaf, sidr, neem, desert date and acacia are grown from seed and cuttings, giving water-wise choices for landscapes and land restoration.

Traceable batches

Every lot is recorded with its source and planting date, so we can tell you where your plants came from and how long they have been grown on.
